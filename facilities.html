Our Hotel Facilities

Grand Core2Dine Hotel offers a wide range of premium facilities designed to provide our guests with comfort, relaxation, and memorable experiences.

1. Swimming Pool

Take a refreshing swim in our outdoor swimming pool, equipped with clean water, relaxing poolside loungers, and dedicated towels.

  • Open daily: 6:00 AM - 9:00 PM
  • Lifeguard on duty during operating hours
  • Dedicated shallow pool area for children

2. Fitness Gym

Stay active and energised throughout your stay in our fully equipped fitness centre.

  • Open 24 hours for all hotel guests
  • Treadmills, elliptical cross trainers, and exercise cycles
  • Dumbbells, weight benches, and strength training equipment
  • Complimentary fitness towels and drinking water

3. Spa and Wellness Center

Relax and rejuvenate your body and mind with our range of holistic therapies and soothing wellness treatments.

  • Full body massage therapies and aromatherapy
  • Invigorating steam room and sauna sessions
  • Certified and experienced wellness practitioners
  • Timings: 9:00 AM - 8:00 PM

4. Core2Dine Multi-Cuisine Restaurant

Enjoy freshly prepared gourmet meals from Indian, Continental, and international cuisines crafted by professional chefs.

  • Extensive breakfast, lunch, and dinner menus
  • Delicious vegetarian, non-vegetarian, and vegan choices
  • 24x7 in-room dining service
View Restaurant Menu

5. Hotel Lobby & 24x7 Reception

Our spacious, elegant lobby features comfortable guest seating, a helpful travel desk, and express check-in and check-out.

  • 24x7 front desk assistance
  • Secure luggage storage service
  • Concierge and local tour guidance

Additional Services & Amenities

  • High-Speed Wi-Fi: Complimentary wireless internet access in all rooms and public spaces.
  • Airport Pickup & Drop: Comfortable airport transfer service available upon prior booking.
  • Secure Parking: On-premise guarded parking facility with 24x7 security monitoring.
  • Daily Housekeeping: Impeccable cleaning and linen service performed daily.
  • Doctor on Call: Quick medical assistance available for any emergencies.
